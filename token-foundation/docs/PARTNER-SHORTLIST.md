# MARS-X Pool partner shortlist

Research checked: 25 September 2026

No partnership exists yet. The contacts below are public business channels; outreach requires the owner's approval and must describe MARS-X Pool honestly as a beta prototype.

## Primary target — Omanhash / Frontier Technology LLC, Oman

**Why it fits:** Omanhash describes itself as Oman's first national Bitcoin mining pool and exposes API keys, watcher links, data export, real-time hashrate and performance data. That makes a read-only mobile monitoring connector a concrete integration proposal rather than a generic sponsorship request. Its site says the service is operated by Frontier Technology LLC.

**Proposed pilot:** official read-only API access for 10–20 authorised miners; MARS-X displays worker availability, hashrate and alerts without holding wallets, changing payout addresses or mining on the phone.

**Public channel:** `support@omanhash.om`. Ask support to route the message to the technical partnerships/API team. Website: https://omanhash.om/

**Do not claim:** that MARS-X is approved by Omanhash, the Omani government or any regulator.

## Secondary target — Green Data City, Oman

**Why it fits:** Green Data City states that it operates Oman's first sustainable crypto-mining licence and allows industrial mining companies to register and operate in its Salalah development. It is a plausible pilot-customer and operator-introduction partner.

**Proposed pilot:** branded fleet-status mobile dashboard for one authorised site, with read-only monitoring first.

**Public channel:** https://www.greendatacity.com/contact — the company's public site also lists `olivier@altaher.com` for enquiries.

## Enterprise target — Phoenix Group, UAE

**Why it fits:** Phoenix says it operates more than 500 MW of mining/digital infrastructure across six markets, including Oman, and its enquiry form includes a dedicated “Partnership Inquiries” category.

**Proposed pilot:** a small internal proof of concept for read-only fleet health and incident alerts. Phoenix is a large enterprise, so approach only after a working demo and security document are ready.

**Public channel:** https://phoenixgroupuae.com/contact — choose “Partnership Inquiries”. Public office number: `+971 4 323 9120`.

## Contact priority

1. Omanhash — best product/API fit.
2. Green Data City — best route to licensed Oman operators.
3. Phoenix Group — highest scale, but least likely to consider a prototype without a strong demo.
