# MARS-X Pool tester and user acquisition plan

Date: 25 September 2026

## Safe positioning

MARS-X Pool is a remote node/pool management project. It does not mine on the Android device and must not promise guaranteed income, passive profit or a fixed return. Until useful remote management is complete, public copy must call it a closed beta/prototype.

## Closed-test campaign

Invite 20 genuine testers so at least 12 remain opted in continuously for 14 days. Start with personal/professional contacts and relevant developer or mining-operations communities where invitations are allowed. Never buy testers, fake activity or reviews.

Each tester should join through the Play closed-test URL, remain opted in for 14 uninterrupted days, test health/licence/register/heartbeat/feedback functions, and submit at least one genuine observation.

## Paid launch only after production readiness

Do not spend on install ads until production access, a privacy/landing page, useful remote-management features, accurate screenshots and measurable onboarding exist.

| Setting | Initial value |
| --- | --- |
| Objective | Qualified app installs |
| Monthly cap | USD 10 total; owner approval required before spend |
| Markets | India first, Indonesia second; Oman/UAE use direct B2B outreach |
| Languages | English, Indonesian, Arabic, Turkish |
| Conversion | Install → onboarding → licensed active user |
| Stop rule | Pause below 15% activation after 100 installs or after any policy failure |

Do not divide the USD 10 budget between countries. Test one English creative in India, pause, improve onboarding, and use the remaining budget only for the winning message. Treat Oman and UAE as direct partnership markets rather than consumer-install campaigns.

Headlines: “MARS-X Pool Closed Beta”, “Remote Mining Node Monitor”, “Monitor Nodes, Not Your Phone”, “Pantau Node dari Jarak Jauh”, “مراقبة العقد عن بُعد”.

Descriptions:

- Uzaktaki node'larını tek panelden izle. Telefonda madencilik yok, kazanç garantisi yok.
- Pantau node jarak jauh. Tidak ada penambangan di ponsel dan tidak ada jaminan penghasilan.
- راقب العقد عن بُعد. لا تعدين على الهاتف ولا ضمان للأرباح.
- Monitor remote nodes from one beta client. No on-device mining and no profit promise.

Measure invitation source, opt-in, install, onboarding, activation, day-1/day-7 retention and submitted feedback. Use aggregated Play Console data first; do not add attribution/advertising SDKs until the privacy notice and consent flow are ready.
