# Unauthorised copy and licence abuse playbook

This checklist is not a promise of automatic legal action.

1. Preserve lawful evidence: original source version, release signing certificate, build/archive hashes, dated screenshots, store URL, suspected APK/hash, licence audit event and purchase record.
2. Verify ownership: contributors, third-party dependencies, commissioned-work assignments and trademark position.
3. Revoke only the compromised entitlement; do not block unrelated users.
4. Send a factual cease-and-desist notice identifying the work, alleged unauthorised act, evidence, requested cure and response deadline.
5. Use the platform's copyright/trademark process only where evidence supports it.
6. For material commercial infringement, consult a French IP lawyer or commissaire de justice before acting in a way that could destroy evidence.
7. Keep logs proportionate and within the published retention period. Do not collect extra personal data merely to identify a suspected copier.

Before launch, deposit the source and documentation with INPI e-Soleau for a certain creation date, retain signed commits and release-certificate fingerprints, consider trademark registration after clearance, use Play App Signing, keep secrets outside the repository, put sensitive logic on the backend, and add Play Integrity plus server-side Play Billing verification.
